## Practice

Between events I work through daily and archive challenges. I write up the ones that teach me something:

- **[AlpacaHack Daily]({{ '/blog/' | relative_url }}#alpacahack-daily):** pwn, rev, crypto and web, one challenge at a time
- **[crackmes.one]({{ '/blog/' | relative_url }}#crackmes-one):** Windows and Linux reverse engineering, anti-analysis and malware-style puzzles
- **CryptoHack:** introduction, modular arithmetic, symmetric and public-key tracks
- **[Hack The Box]({{ site.social.htb }}):** labs, Sherlocks and Academy modules

## Toolbox

- **Reverse engineering:** IDA, Ghidra, x64dbg, radare2, angr, Capstone, pefile
- **Exploitation:** pwntools, GDB with GEF, ROPgadget, Docker for matching target libc versions
- **DFIR:** Volatility 3, Wireshark/tshark, MFTECmd, RegRipper, Autopsy, bulk_extractor, `Get-WinEvent`
- **Web and offensive:** Burp Suite, ffuf, nmap, hashcat, John the Ripper

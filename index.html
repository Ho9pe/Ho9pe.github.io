About

I graduated from Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology (RUET) in August 2026, ranked 14th in my cohort with a CGPA of 3.79/4.00. I work where cybersecurity meets machine learning. My undergraduate thesis built graph-neural-network malware detectors from binary control flow graphs, then tested them on malware collected a year later. The model that looked best on the usual benchmark was among the first to break, and that finding is now a journal paper under review.

Before the thesis I led a four-person undergraduate research group whose work on satellite imagery, clinical text and medical imaging produced three IEEE conference papers. Outside research I analyse malware, reverse engineer binaries and play CTFs, mostly solo on Hack The Box, and I write up the challenges I learn the most from. At the moment I'm working through Practical Malware Analysis and Hands-On Machine Learning.

I'm looking for fully funded PhD positions starting in 2027. I'm also open to roles in security research, machine learning and software engineering.

Last month I took part in **HTB Cyber Apocalypse CTF 2026** as a solo player. I solved **120 of 136 challenges** and finished **210th out of 6,744 teams**.

It was an intense week, but the challenges were fun and elegant, and I learned a lot. I enjoyed the **Web** and **Secure Coding** categories the most. *Web/Provision* was the most rewarding solve: a PHP object injection that I had to turn into a working POP gadget chain by hand.

Thanks to Hack The Box for putting together such a well-designed CTF.
